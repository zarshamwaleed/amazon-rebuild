## Design Brief — Avenzo Homepage v2

**1. Editorial angle: "The Archive."**
Avenzo's palette (bone/stone paper tones, brass hardware accent, charcoal ink) and existing Fraunces serif already read like a bound catalog, not an app — so the concept treats the homepage as an index into a curated archive of goods: numbered entries, ledger-style facts, a foil-brass stamp instead of a UI accent color. It's the one angle that uses what's already in the token system as *content*, not just decoration.

**2. Typography.**
Keep the existing three-family system but assign each a deliberate editorial role instead of a generic UI role:
- **Fraunces** — display headlines and pull-quotes, set large, often italic, sometimes overlapping section boundaries. This is the "brand voice."
- **Inter** — body copy and nav only, kept quiet and small so it never competes with Fraunces.
- **JetBrains Mono** — repurposed as the archive's numbering/metadata system: catalog numbers ("N° 001"), dates, section indices, stat labels. Currently tagged "technical contexts only" in the design system — here it becomes a visible brand device (foil-stamp numerals), which is a more specific use than "monospace for code."

No new typeface introduced; the pairing is justified by giving mono a real editorial job instead of leaving it invisible.

**3. Color/contrast plan.**
Bone/stone stay the paper ground for ~80% of the page. Charcoal-900 is used as a full inverted band exactly once (the brand-story pull-quote) for scale contrast against all the light sections around it. Brass is rationed to single moments per section — one stamped numeral, one underline, one CTA — never a background wash or repeated button color. Deliberate breaks from even spacing: the hero headline column and image column are unequal widths (not 50/50), one product image bleeds off the viewport edge, and one headline overlaps downward into the section below it.

**4. Section-by-section layout.**
- **Hero** — asymmetric split, not centered: ~40% column of oversized Fraunces headline (left, text-anchored, small mono "N° 001 — SS26" stamp above it), ~60% image bleeding off the right viewport edge, uncropped by any card frame. CTA is a text link ("Enter the Archive →"), not a button.
- **Index rail** — a thin fixed vertical strip (desktop only) listing the sections as numbered entries (01 Collections … 05 Ledger) that fills brass as each section scrolls into view — the signature interaction, table-of-contents-as-navigation rather than a hover-lift.
- **Featured Collections** — irregular bento grid: one tall dominant tile plus varied-size tiles, captions run as a mono-numbered list beneath rather than icon+label cards.
- **Editor's Picks** — single oversized spotlight product whose image overlaps upward into the previous section, huge price/name typeset beside it, followed by a horizontal shelf for the rest (magazine spread, not a uniform grid).
- **Trending** — ranked two-column list where the rank numbers themselves are large italic Fraunces glyphs, not icons or badges.
- **Brand Story** — full charcoal-900 inverted band, an oversized edge-to-edge Fraunces italic pull-quote wider than a normal reading measure, with a small asymmetrically-placed inset image (not a full-bleed background photo).
- **Trust / "Ledger"** — reframed as a thin-ruled horizontal fact table (founded year, sourcing, artisans, returns) in mono+serif, replacing any icon-row pattern.
- **Closing / Brand Promise** — an asymmetric closing statement whose headline overlaps into the footer space, two plain text-link CTAs, no card.

Motion stays restrained: the index rail's scroll-spy fill is the primary interaction; the hero gets a slow parallax; nothing else uses a uniform fade-up-on-scroll treatment.
